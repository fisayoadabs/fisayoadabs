## 👋 About Me

Hey! I'm **Fizzy** — a Software Engineering grad from the **University of Calgary** with a Minor in Mechatronics. Passionate about building embedded systems, full-stack platforms, and AI-integrated products that make an impact and solve real-world problems. If it's at the intersection of hardware and software, I'm probably building it. Always exploring new technologies.

- 🔧 **Embedded Software** — BLE firmware (ESP32, C++), UART protocols
- 📱 **Mobile & Full-Stack** — iOS (Swift), Android (Kotlin), React, Next.js, TypeScript
- 🤖 **Robotics** — ROS2 control systems, rover wireless comms (Schulich Space Rover Team)
- 🥽 **AR/VR & 3D** — Three.js, WebXR, ArcGIS, interactive geospatial platforms
- 🧠 **AI** — LLMs, LangChain, Llama API, AI-powered data pipelines
- 📬 Open to collaborations and new opportunities
